# PRODUCT — Genfundet

Danish direct-to-consumer service: upload a phone photo of a damaged family photograph, see it restored in
about 20 seconds, order it restored, printed and framed (30×40 cm) for 599 kr. incl. shipping.
One product, one price. The customer approves the finished image by email before anything is printed.

Audience: Danish adults 35–65, on a phone, arriving from a Meta ad, often thinking about a parent or grandparent.
Success this week: 1.500 kr. of cold traffic produces at least one verified purchase.
Brand commitments: Nordic editorial (DESIGN.md), no fake proof (ANTI_SLOP.md), copy locked in lib/copy.ts.
